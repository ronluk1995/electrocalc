Privacy Policy — ElectroCalc 1.0

Effective Date: 2026

1. Overview

ElectroCalc 1.0 ("the App") is an offline-capable electrical calculation utility. We respect your privacy and designed the App to perform all mathematical and electrical calculations directly on your device.

2. Data Collection & Storage

ElectroCalc 1.0 does not require user registration and does not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, location, or contacts) on external servers.

Any preferences you choose inside the App (such as language or decimal precision) remain strictly local on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Future or ad-supported versions of the App may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob / Google AdSense). These providers may use anonymous device identifiers or cookies in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve non-personalized or personalized ads depending on your consent settings.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
